## Mission connection

The point of this pack is the same principle behind PreserveMy.World's actual archive: a reconstruction, or in this case a body of work, is only as trustworthy as how easy it is to independently check. A 3D model nobody can verify against its source footage is just an unverifiable claim wearing a heritage site's shape. This pack applies that same standard to my own internship record: every claim of "I built this" here has a real, independently-checkable link sitting next to it, not a summary asking to be trusted.
